Welcome to the Kloud Of Trust - the cloud-native, role-based organization of coders and testers. At present, it is my hobby project with three more hobby projects in it:

  • Stellar TESTNET Monitor,
  • Cloudflare Job Fair, and
  • Stellar Help Exchange.

Stellar TESTNET Monitor listens for Job Requests from Job Users and queues them when needed. Cloudflare Job Fair matches Job Agents (digital content producers) with Job Users (digital content consumers) for a given job and facilitates the data flow between them. Stellar Help Exchange provides an equitable marketplace, where job users pay job agents for jobs done - or get full refund otherwise.

I believe these are the first steps needed to make the Internet a better place for you and me. So, if you are a coder or a tester - join me! As a coder, you'll be rewarded by the users of your code. As a tester, you'll get paid for your resources.
