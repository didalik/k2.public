Dear Reader,

Welcome to the Kloud Of Trust - the cloud-native, role-based organization of coders and testers. At present, it is my hobby project with three more hobby projects in it:

  • Stellar TESTNET Monitor,
  • Cloudflare Job Fair, and
  • Stellar Help Exchange.

Stellar TESTNET Monitor listens for Job Requests from Job Users and gates them as needed using Stellar DEX trades. When a Job Request is granted, the monitor starts the corresponding Job Agent. This serverless solution gives you a 24/7 listener behind your Internet Service Provider. See my k2.hx GitHub repo for details.

Cloudflare Job Fair matches Job Agents (digital content producers) with Job Users (digital content consumers) for a given Job and facilitates the data flow between them. I employ Hibernation WebSocket API to implement a Match → Relay handshake that pairs up two sockets (a Job Offer from the Job Agent and a Job Request from the Job User) and then relays messages between the paired. Please find the SDK here.

Stellar Help Exchange provides an equitable marketplace, where job users pay job agents for jobs done - or get full refund otherwise. My k2.hx GitHub repo has more details - sequence diagrams, API and SDK.

I believe these are the first steps needed to make the Internet a better place for you and me. So, if you are a coder or a tester - consider joining me on GitHub! As a coder, you'll be rewarded by the users of your code. As a tester, you'll get paid for your resources.
